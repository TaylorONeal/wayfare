# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful and constructive. Harassment and discrimination are not
tolerated. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate
this code, and may ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

Report concerns by opening a confidential issue or contacting a maintainer listed
in the repository. Reports will be handled discreetly.
